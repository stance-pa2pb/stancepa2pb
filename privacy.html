Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Last updated: September 2026

This is a plain-language starting policy for our pre-launch stage. As STANCE PA2PB grows, this page will be reviewed and formalised with proper legal guidance.

What we collect

When you join our waitlist as a rider or driver, we collect your full name, email address, WhatsApp number, city, and — for drivers — your car details and build style. We only collect what's needed to match riders with crews and keep you updated.

Why we collect it

  • To contact you on WhatsApp or email when the app launches
  • To match riders with nearby, vetted stance crews
  • To review and verify driver signups before approving them

How we store it

Your information is stored securely using Supabase, a hosted database provider with industry-standard security practices. Access to view this data is restricted to the STANCE PA2PB founder only, through a password-protected dashboard.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties. Email and WhatsApp messaging is handled through trusted service providers solely to deliver messages to you.

Your rights

You can request to see, correct, or delete your information at any time. In line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you have the right to know what we hold about you and to ask us to remove it.

Contact us

For any privacy questions or to request your data be removed, reach out via WhatsApp at 067 950 3097 or email kabelosiyabonga2@gmail.com.

Terms of use

STANCE PA2PB is currently in a pre-launch, manual-matching phase. Rides booked via WhatsApp are arranged directly between riders and drivers; payment is made directly to the driver. STANCE PA2PB is not yet a licensed transport operator and does not guarantee ride availability during this phase.
